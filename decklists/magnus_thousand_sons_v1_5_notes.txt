Magnus the Red — Thousand Sons Conclave v1.5: Changes and Analysis
October 27, 2026

CHANGES FROM V1.4
ADD  Jace, Vryn's Prodigy        CUT  Kitsa, Otterball Elite
ADD  Goblin Electromancer       CUT  Stormcatch Mentor
ADD  Sigil Tracer              CUT  Whispers of the Muse
ADD  Cryptic Gateway           CUT  Scroll Rack

Counts: 1 commander, 23 creatures, 14 artifacts/enchantments, 27 instants/sorceries, 35 lands = 100.
All six Tzeentch creatures remain: Tzaangor Shaman, Exalted Flamer of Tzeentch, Pink Horror, Heralds of Tzeentch, Lord of Change, Mutalith Vortex Beast. Magnus and The Lost and the Damned remain.
Stormsplitter is the only Otter creature in the deck.

WHAT THIS GAINS
- Sigil Tracer copies any instant/sorcery (including X-spells) as an activated Wizard ability. Cost 1U plus tapping two untapped Wizards. This is independent of Magnus. Sigil Tracer can tap itself, even on the turn it enters.
- Cryptic Gateway puts a creature card from hand directly onto battlefield, using two untapped creatures that share a creature type with it. Arcane Adaptation choosing Wizard, or Maskwood Nexus, makes the expensive Tzeentch creatures in hand Wizards. Newly created creatures can be tapped toward Gateway immediately.
- Jace costs 1U, draws then discards on tapping; useful for developing graveyard and improving draws. If transformed, can cast a target instant or sorcery from graveyard once through planeswalker loyalty ability. It is no longer a Wizard on its planeswalker face.
- Goblin Electromancer costs UR and reduces generic instant and sorcery costs by 1, stacking with Magnus. Helps generic buyback costs.

WHAT IT COSTS
- Whispers of the Muse was a cheap one-card cantrip with repeatable buyback, so spell density moves from 28 to 27. Its removal reduces card-flow redundancy.
- Scroll Rack was repeatable hand-to-library setup for Chaos Mutation; now Brainstorm is the primary precise hand-to-top setup. Ponder can arrange the top cards already in library. Underworld Breach enables graveyard Brainstorm at a resource cost.
- Cryptic Gateway is a five-mana artifact, and the deck now has one additional late-game setup piece. Its utility requires creatures in hand and compatible bodies; it is not an early mana source.
- Kitsa's activated copying is replaced by Sigil Tracer; unlike Kitsa, Sigil Tracer needs two Wizards, but doesn't need a power threshold.
- Goblin Electromancer lacks Stormcatch Mentor's haste/prowess, but both discount instants/sorceries identically.

COMBOS TO TEST
1. Turn-one/four ramp: can we cast Magnus by turn 4-5 without sacrificing early interaction?
2. Wizard setup: how often do we access 2+ Wizards (or type converter) to use Patron Wizard, Voidmage Prodigy, Sigil Tracer, and Cryptic Gateway?
3. Demon summoning: cryptic gateway needs a creature card in hand and 2 compatible creatures in play. With Arcane Adaptation/Maskwood Nexus, any tokens count as Wizards. Without them, 2 Demons can summon a Demon, or Wizards summon an actual Wizard.
4. Underworld Breach can cast a spell from graveyard if escape paid (mana plus exiling three other cards); The Lost and the Damned rewards this cast, but not ordinary hand casting or putting a creature onto battlefield via Gateway.
5. Mystic Speculation buyback loop: Requires a sustainable generic discount and mana/treasure/creature converter; verify actual battlefield requirements each run.
6. Searing Touch buyback + Storm-Kiln Artist can generate repeated damage if generic buyback paid/discounted and each cast's Treasure pays R.
7. Mathemagics X=7 and spell copying: Sigil Tracer can copy, retaining X=7; two copies or additional targets are required for a three-opponent table. Sigil Tracer's activated cost is NOT discounted by Magnus.
