# V25 assessment

Personal Tutor is a U sorcery that searches for a sorcery, with an optional fail-to-find, reveal, shuffle and top-of-library placement. Seven tests cover search restrictions, timing through priority choices, payment, Pyromancer and tutor-to-Reinforcements. A source-paid narrative casts both spells and observes four Elementals.

All 666 tests passed locally. A 200-game v1.13 sweep across all seats passed consistency checks with zero safety stops. Median eight-round runtime was 318.28 ms over 12 games. Host and game trajectory differences mean this is exploratory, not a controlled comparison. Existing caches are unchanged.

Search selection is heuristic by default; many other tutors, card effects, simultaneous token-entry rules and graveyard mechanics remain incomplete. Results do not establish deck strength.
