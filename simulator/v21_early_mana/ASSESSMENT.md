# V21 assessment

Frostboil Snarl supports optional reveal of an Island or Mountain card in hand, tapped entry otherwise and U/R mana. The land is identified before initial draws and mulligan checks. A source-paid narrative reveals Island and uses Snarl to cast Mystical Tutor for Consider. The first ten focused checks include Magnus reduction with Capsize buyback and a noncreature-token exclusion.

All 629 tests passed locally. The 200-game 12-round v1.13 sweep passed consistency checks across four seats with zero safety stops. Median eight-round runtime was 324.504 ms over 12 games, an exploratory measurement affected by host and game trajectories. Existing caches remain unchanged.

Most of the deck still needs implementation. Other mana sources, Sapphire Medallion, Springleaf Drum, remaining token engines, tutor chains, copies, graveyard casting and Phyrexian Altar remain open. Results are not deck-strength estimates.
