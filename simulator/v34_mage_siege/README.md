# V34 Mage Siege Adventure

Lindblum may be played as a tapped red-producing land or cast from hand as the 2R instant Mage Siege. On resolution Mage Siege creates a black 0/1 Wizard token and exiles the physical Lindblum card on Adventure; that same card may later be played as a land from exile using the usual land timing and land-play limit. A countered Adventure goes to the graveyard. The Wizard's ability triggers when its controller casts a noncreature spell and deals one damage to each opponent; Harmonic Prodigy doubles it. Reiterate can copy Mage Siege, creating another token, without casting the copy.

This is a card-specific Adventure implementation, not a general Adventure framework. Arbitrary effects interacting with exile, comprehensive replacement or prevention, and complete land timing rules remain incomplete. See the interaction narrative and coverage audit. Reference: [FINAL FANTASY release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/final-fantasy-release-notes).
