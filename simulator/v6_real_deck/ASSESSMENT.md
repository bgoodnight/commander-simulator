# v6 integration assessment — October 28, 2026

## What was tested

**Goal:** Verify that a real 99-card Magnus v1.6 library and commander zone can occupy a normal player seat in the existing four-player v5 abstract engine. This is a **vertical integration proof**, NOT a real-deck performance study. No Magnus card substitutions were made; no Monte Carlo win rates were calculated.

**Four narrated games:** three unforced opening hands (seeds 102, 105, 103) and one intentionally **forced opening** (seed 404) to exercise token, commander, mana, and response behavior. All narratives stop at round 10; longer test-only continuations of three same-seed matchups were inspected separately (rounds 15, 20, and 16) but are not deck-performance estimates.

**Automated tests:** 21 v6; also 21 unchanged v5, 15 unchanged v4, 20 unchanged v3 passed.

### Positive evidence from the logs

- Mana is generated by actual land/rock names, and **U/R requirements matter**. A Mountain cannot pay Counterspell's {U}{U}; Sol Ring produces two generic/colorless, not blue. Izzet Signet requires another mana to activate. Blue/red mana and colored Treasures are consumed without double-tapping.
- The commander begins outside the library; one additional cast from the command zone costs two more generic mana. If removed or countered, it can be recast.
- Typed creature, instant, sorcery, artifact, and counterspell actions all use the same v5 table/player interfaces and actual opposing creature targets. Pongify's victim is destroyed and its controller receives a 3/3 Ape.
- Instant/sorcery cast triggers occur *after* mana cost is fixed, including when the spell is countered. Young Pyromancer, Third Path Iconoclast, Storm-Kiln Artist, and Harmonic Prodigy interact in the shared board state. Stormsplitter copies disappear at the next end step.
- Magnus is a 4/5 flying creature. It produces a 3/3 Spawn after unblocked combat damage, and its discount only removes **generic** mana from instants/sorceries. Synthetic opponent creatures can sometimes have flying; ground blockers cannot block Magnus.
- Opponents can remove Magnus, force a recast, and cast spells that Magnus counters. The expanded detailed pilot does not intentionally throw token/draw engines into hopeless attacks.
- A player may die individually while the table continues. For example, in seed 105 the Magnus seat is eliminated by combat on round nine and the remaining three continue.

## Coverage and serious limitations

**Library:** exactly 99 cards, with Magnus in the command zone. There are 82 unique names in the full deck, 18 land names with mana-source representations, 26 supported nonland library cards plus Magnus, and **38 other unique nonland cards currently unsupported**. Unsupported cards can be drawn and seen, but **the pilot cannot play them**. They are not covertly substituted with generic creatures/spells.

Current named subset includes: the commander; selected lands and six mana rocks; Young Pyromancer, Third Path Iconoclast, Storm-Kiln Artist, Harmonic Prodigy, Goblin Electromancer, Baral, Stormsplitter, Archmage Emeritus; selected cantrips; Counterspell, Swan Song, Arcane Denial, An Offer You Can't Refuse and Flusterstorm; and Pongify. Several spells (including the selection spells and nontrivial counters) have approximated effects.

**Not yet supported:** the six Warhammer/Tzeentch creatures' specific bodies/text, Patron Wizard tax and Voidmage Prodigy sacrifice counter, Sigil Tracer, exact card selection, tutors, spell copies, buyback, graveyard casting, precise timing/priority, finite spell stack, complex mana/land utilities, detailed blocking/keyword set, commander-damage tracking, and Magnus's actual combo win recipes. Specific lands have common ETB conditions, but utility lands and painlands are approximated. The abstract opponent economy remains its calibrated v5 approximation.

**Implication:** In seed 105 Magnus has no playable win line despite a recognizable token board and loses under aggression; this is principally a card-coverage and pilot-readiness diagnostic, not evidence that the actual v1.6 deck loses that matchup. Likewise v6 outcomes or game lengths should **not** be compared with the v5 abstract archetype win rates.

## Issues uncovered and engineering response

1. **v5 fungible mana** was insufficient for actual Commander costs. Added an injectable colored payment policy (without modifying v5) that handles separate land and mana-rock modes, locked-in generic discounts, Izzet Signet's activation tax, floating Sol Ring mana and single-use colored Treasures.
2. **v5 response window** consumed generic counters without considering colored mana. Added a mixed-table response handler that consumes genuine named counterspell cards, pays colored mana, emits cast triggers and observes spell types. Arbitrary priority and full counter-counter chains still need future work.
3. **v5 generic creature combat** had no flying and carelessly exposed engine creatures. Added flying legality for mixed games and a protective Magnus pilot. The abstract baseline remains frozen.
4. **v3 named-card status is not the same as executable v5 rules.** Added an explicit reviewed subset, an inert unsupported category, and a coverage ledger (`coverage_casts`, `unsupported_held`, and `known_rules_gaps`).
5. **Visible-board threat isn't true win proximity.** Unchanged from v5 for this pass; future pilot threat choices and real card win conditions must improve before deck comparisons.
