# Magnus v1.13 budget — development roadmap

The canonical input is `decklists/magnus_thousand_sons_v1_13_budget.txt`. Use `BudgetTable` and this exact list for new integration and performance measurements; preserve historical versioned engines and v14 fingerprints for regression and later caching checks. Supported-subset labels are not Oracle certifications. Do not infer deck strength from the current partial model.

## Ordered milestones

1. **Card data and legal deck baseline.** Verify all 82 distinct names against an authoritative card catalog, including full type lines, mana costs, color identities, split or double-faced characteristics and commander legality. Verify 100 cards and singleton exceptions. Record provenance. Replace the 29 unknown-type placeholders with verified descriptors, while keeping unsupported effects inert. Re-enable Mystical Tutor only when it can search the complete legal candidate set. Treat Phyrexian Altar as a normal card regardless of physical proxy status.
2. **Mana, cost and early turns.** Verify v1.13 land behavior and mana sources, including Frostboil Snarl, Cascade Bluffs, Exotic Orchard, Springleaf Drum and Sapphire Medallion. Test Magnus, Goblin Electromancer and Baral with each other and with colored costs and additional costs. Implement the deck's other cost reducers as verified. Compare early land drops and source-paid plays; preserve priorities and payment decisions explicitly.
3. **Early draw and token engines.** Add a focused batch from Consider, Faithless Looting, Gitaxian Probe, Hard Evidence, Ral's Reinforcements and Strike It Rich. Then implement Improbable Alliance, Saheeli and Talrand, including correct token characteristics and timing. Test the tokens with Magnus, existing Young Pyromancer and Storm-Kiln Artist. Model color where needed rather than assuming existing Spirit tokens are red.
4. **Tutors and search chains.** Complete and test Mystical Tutor for this deck; add Merchant Scroll, Personal Tutor, Solve the Equation, Fabricate, Gamble, Muddle the Mixture's transmute, Step Through's wizardcycling, Spellseeker and Imperial Recruiter in small batches. Enforce each card's actual search restrictions, destinations, reveal/shuffle, activation timing and associated costs. Test realistic early chains and fail-to-find; default selection policy remains a heuristic until explicitly evaluated.
5. **Buyback, convoke and reusable spells.** Extend the existing Capsize subset to the actual buyback cards Searing Touch, Fanning the Flames, Spell Burst and Whispers of the Muse; cover alternative costs, X choices, reductions, countered spells and return destinations. Add convoke for Artistic Refusal and Meeting of Minds, including legal tap choices, colored payments and sequencing. Test with Magnus and token makers.
6. **Wizard/Shaman and copy interactions.** Add Veyran and relevant remaining Wizard/Shaman effects, test with Harmonic Prodigy, triggers, casts and copied spells. Build stack-copy primitives before Increasing Vengeance and Dualcaster Mage; verify copied spell targets and magecraft semantics, including that copies are not casts. Cover responses and source removal.
7. **Graveyard, sacrifice and combo paths.** Implement Phyrexian Altar's sacrifice-for-mana, Underworld Breach's escape and sacrifice timing, and graveyard casting/recovery for deck cards such as Faithless Looting and Strike It Rich. Model interactions with Capsize and Mystic Sanctuary. Validate bounded, legal tutor-to-engine and finisher sequences, including Crackle with Power; no infinite-loop claim without a demonstrated legal loop and resource accounting.
8. **Remaining v1.13 cards and game rules.** Triage remaining unsupported deck cards, interaction effects and finishing paths by impact on real sequences. Address Commander-specific rules, opponents and decision quality before drawing deck-strength conclusions. Keep removed cards, such as Otawara, Stormsplitter and Arcane Adaptation, as historical regression coverage rather than leading new deck-specific development.
9. **Performance after correctness.** Measure current v1.13 engine with fixed seeds and recorded environments after milestones; retain v14 historical fingerprints without comparing different deck trajectories as identical. Defer additional caching until functional baselines are stable, then compare cached and uncached outputs under the same deck, rules, choices and seeds.

Each milestone needs targeted tests, at least representative source-paid narratives and checks of zone conservation, stack timing and payment as applicable. Update the README and v1.13 coverage audit, report limitations, and commit after checks pass. Avoid claiming full rules support from a passing smoke sweep.
